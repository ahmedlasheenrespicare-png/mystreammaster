1

Pick a package & confirm on WhatsApp

Choose the server that fits your usage, then complete your order via WhatsApp. Our team confirms the package and the payment method available in your country (mada, Visa, Apple Pay, STC Pay and more).

2

Receive your provider-issued details

Within minutes you receive the subscription data issued by the external provider — an M3U link and Xtream Codes credentials — via WhatsApp and email, with setup guidance.

We set up your player together

Install a player on your Samsung, LG, Android, Apple TV, Fire TV Stick or computer, enter your details, and we tune the quality to your connection speed, step by step.
